# Surface: hikayacoffee.ca + shop (Astro, /en and /ar)

Mode: Persuade (home, Ramadan, Eid) with Operate sub-surfaces (shop, cart, checkout) and Read (/brew).
Audience: Arab families in Calgary and curious Calgarians, equally. Action: pre-order coffee + dates for pickup/delivery in Calgary.
Proof on hand: real packaging system, films, studio box shots, Calgary scenes. No testimonials; guest lines are labelled samples.
Constraints: brand book v0.8 palette/fonts/voice (PRODUCT.md). Incumbent look = client mode of team.hikayacoffee.ca and the packaging. Degraded direction roll (no challengers); user delegated the choice and asked to stay true to the brand.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The website is the package, opened. It refuses the coffee-roaster default (full-bleed lifestyle hero + product grid of photos) and instead wears the real pouch and box: bubble-pattern paper, a seasonal sleeve band, and products shown as their actual front sticker.
OWN-WORLD: Paper ground carrying the cream-conversation bubble pattern; solid Tamr / Khalal / Bunn sleeve bands that own full-width regions; code-drawn 250 g pouches with the 80×80 sticker (bubble icon, AR name in El Messiri, EN caps, spice dots, roast bar); speech-bubble shapes (tail corner) as the only container shape; Arabic-first pairs.
STORY: Visitor hears Bunn and Tamr talk, sees coffee always arrive with a date, picks a pair, understands one box with three insides and three sleeves, and pre-orders for pickup Thursday–Sunday or Calgary delivery.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Khalal sleeve band under the header carrying the season line. Left 55%: the hero is a Bunn bubble and a Tamr bubble at display scale (Arabic first, English under), CTA pair below (Shop · Pre-order Ramadan). Right 45%: a code-drawn pouch standing beside the real date box photo on paper. Pattern only around, never behind the logo.
FORM: Packaging-world inheritance with hakawati chapter numerals (raise from candidate 1) and an imsakiya iftar table on Ramadan (candidate 3, the dealt card). Seed key 70ffab09.
SIGNATURE: The pairing picker: choose a coffee, Tamr answers with a date in a bubble and both go to the cart together.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
