There is no separate "soft open" before Ramadan: the Coming soon page and its waitlist are the
build-up, and the site opens straight into Ramadan pre-orders.

## What opens phase 2: the paperwork

Pre-orders open only when these are done (details and who to ask: `docs/launch-plan.md`, section 5):

- Alberta Health Services food handling permit for where coffee is ground and dates are portioned,
  and a food safety course for whoever handles food.
- City of Calgary business licence.
- Product liability insurance.
- Dates: buy from a Canadian importer, or hold a CFIA Safe Food for Canadians licence to import.
- Labels checked: English and French, ingredients and allergens, net quantity, best before, lot.
- GST answer from the accountant (the site says "GST pending" until then).

Then the launch-day steps in `docs/launch-plan.md` section 9 (final prices, Square, domain, one
real test order, then `SITE_PUBLIC=true`).

## Each phase

**1. Coming soon + waitlist**
- Site: done. Sign-ups show on Today ("on the mailing list") and in Shop → Promotions, with how many
  came from the Coming soon page.
- Instagram (@hikaya.yyc): two or three posts a week; who you are, why coffee and dates, the three
  coffee families, one coffee a week as a reel. Every post ends with "link in bio: join the list".
  Bio: "قريباً في كالغاري · Coming soon to Calgary" and the site link.
- You: the paperwork above, final prices, photos, address and hours (Settings → Business details).

**2. Ramadan pre-orders**
- Site: Ramadan switch on (Shop → Products), limits (Settings), order-by deadline, first shifts.
- Email: a letter to the whole list on opening day (Shop → Promotions → Letters, Arabic and English).
- Instagram: countdown stories the week before; one Ramadan box per post; the iftar-times table;
  "order by Thursday for this weekend".

**3. During Ramadan**
- Site: Today every morning; switch on the Eid boxes about halfway (around 22 February) with a clear
  last day to order for Eid.
- Instagram: less selling, more of the table: iftar spreads, pouring qahwa, customers' photos (with
  permission).

**4. Eid**
- Site: Eid deliveries, then both seasons off; the everyday banner returns by itself.
- A thank-you letter to the list; review requests go out after delivery on their own.

**5. After Eid**
- Site: the everyday shop; the monthly report shows what sold; regular orders keep customers coming back.
- Instagram: brewing how-tos, origins, regular customers.
- Plan next: wholesale for cafés and mosques, loyalty, the café and tastings.
